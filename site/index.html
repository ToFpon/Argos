Look at your photos.
Nothing in the way.

Argos shows a folder as a grid of thumbnails, with the folder tree on the side, and opens your photos in a viewer laid out like gThumb. The mouse wheel goes to the next photo, Ctrl + wheel zooms right where you point. Native GTK4 and libadwaita, following your desktop theme.

Browse

A folder at a glance

The photos of the folder in natural order (2 before 10), the folders in the tree beside them. Thumbnails are shared with Nautilus: what your file manager already made shows up instantly, and a folder you come back to is there at once.

Folder tree and properties

  • Home, Pictures, Desktop, Downloads, every mounted disk
  • The tree unfolds by itself to the folder you are in
  • Properties of the selected photo under the tree (I)
  • Sort by name, date or size; the folder is watched, new photos appear
  • Four thumbnail sizes, also with Ctrl + wheel — large ones for tired eyes
  • Select several photos with the mouse, Ctrl or Shift; Ctrl+A for all
  • Opens on the last folder, on Pictures or on a folder of your choice
View

The FastStone way of moving around

Wheel = next or previous photo. Ctrl + wheel = zoom around the pointer. Drag to move a zoomed photo, double click for a plain full screen. Neighbouring photos are read in advance: the next one is already there.

Everything about the shot

  • Thumbnail strip with an adjustable height — one row or many
  • Status bar: 3 / 13 · 1280 × 1271 · 439.7 kB · JPEG · 45%
  • Camera, lens, exposure, aperture, ISO, focal length, location (EXIF)
  • JPEG, PNG, animated GIF, WebP, TIFF, BMP, SVG, HEIC, AVIF

Your theme, light or dark

No colours of its own: Argos follows Adwaita, your accent colour and your GTK4 theme. Only the full screen is black around the photo.

Slideshow, with a gentle slide

F5 and the photos follow one another in full screen, every few seconds. Space pauses, the arrows move on, Escape stops. Each photo glides in — or fades in, as you prefer — and the same transition follows you when you browse with the keyboard or the wheel. In full screen, the arrows and the pointer hide when the mouse rests.

Edit

Just what a photo needs

Rotate, crop, save — or save as another format. The original is kept for a moment: Undo puts it back right after saving.

Crop

Draw the frame, drag its edges and corners, keep the rule of thirds in sight. Free, original or fixed ratios: 1:1, 4:3, 3:2, 16:9 and their portrait versions.

Convert or resize many at once

Select photos in the grid and press Ctrl+E: resize, rotate and convert in one go, next to the originals or in their place, with the progress in the window and a Cancel that really stops.

Annotate

Press A: circle the summit, add an arrow and a word, highlight, blur out what should not be seen. Rectangle, ellipse, arrow, line, freehand, highlighter, pixelate and text, with undo. Saved safely: the original comes back with one click, and the photo keeps its date and camera details.

↻

Rotate

R and Shift+R. The photo is saved the way you see it, EXIF orientation included.

⇄

Save as

JPEG, PNG, WebP, TIFF, BMP, GIF — the extension decides. JPEG quality kept.

⟲

Undo

The original is kept after saving, one click brings it back.

🗑

Trash

Delete moves the photo to the trash, the next one shows up.

🖼

Right-click

Set as wallpaper, print, open with, copy or move to, rename (F2), copy the image.

⚡

Big photos, quickly

HEIC and AVIF sizes are read from the file header: a folder of phone photos opens without decoding them all.

Install

Ready to look.

Argos is available for Ubuntu 24.04 “Noble” and 26.04 “Resolute” and their derivatives — Zorin OS 18, Linux Mint 22 — from a PPA, under X11 or Wayland. The package is called argos-viewer.

Terminal
$ sudo add-apt-repository ppa:nourpon/argos
$ sudo apt update && sudo apt install argos-viewer
# optional: make it your default photo viewer (also a switch in the Preferences)
$ xdg-mime default io.github.argos.Argos.desktop image/jpeg image/png

Welcome

Requirements: Python 3.10+, PyGObject, GTK ≥ 4.14, libadwaita ≥ 1.5 — all installed by the package. Recommended, and installed along: imagemagick (editing), gir1.2-gexiv2-0.10 (shooting details), webp-pixbuf-loader, heif-gdk-pixbuf (WebP, HEIC, AVIF).
